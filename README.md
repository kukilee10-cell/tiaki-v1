# TIAKI V1

I want to start a brand-new app called TIAKI.

This app is completely separate from Rotation and should have its own identity, design language and purpose.

The name comes from the Cook Islands Māori word Tiaki, meaning to care for, protect and look after.

The purpose of the app is to help people manage everyday life without feeling overwhelmed. The philosophy is:

“Set it once. Let Tiaki do the rest.”

Do not reuse any layouts, colours or styling from Rotation.

Create a premium, modern and calming design using soft colours, subtle animations, rounded cards and generous spacing. The experience should feel organised, relaxing and effortless rather than industrial or busy.

Version 1 must remain simple and offline-first.

No login.
No cloud sync.
No subscriptions.
No payments.
No AI.
No family sharing.

All information should be stored locally on the phone with local notifications only.

The home screen should feel like a personal life dashboard rather than a checklist.

Design the interface around cards for important life categories such as:

• Documents
• Vehicles
• Home
• Travel
• Family
• Pets
• Reminders
• Maintenance
• Personal records

The dashboard should quickly show what needs attention today, what is due soon and what is all good.

The goal is not to overwhelm the user with information. Every screen should be simple, intuitive and require as few taps as possible.

Think like both a senior product designer and mobile UX designer. Before making decisions, explain why you chose the layout, how it improves usability and how it supports the long-term vision of TIAKI becoming a premium native app in future while keeping Version 1 intentionally simple.

I want people to open TIAKI and immediately feel organised, calm and in control of their life.
